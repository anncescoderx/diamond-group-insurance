Frequently Asked Questions.

Do I need to know what type of insurance I need before reaching out?

No. You can start with your questions, your priorities, and what you hope to protect. A licensed professional can help explain relevant options and limitations.

Can I ask about life insurance and retirement-related products?

Yes. You can share what you want to understand. Product availability, suitability, costs, risks, and guarantees vary, so details should be reviewed with the appropriately licensed professional.

Is the first conversation a commitment to buy?

No. An initial conversation can be an opportunity to ask questions and understand whether you want to explore further.

Do I need experience to explore becoming an agent?

You can ask about the path even if you are new to the industry. Licensing requirements, training, qualifications, and business expectations should be discussed before making a decision.

What should I ask before joining the team?

Ask about licensing, training, supervision, support, compensation, expenses, lead generation, and what a typical working week looks like. Make sure you understand the terms before committing.

Is income guaranteed for agents?

No income or results are guaranteed. Earnings, if any, can vary based on licensing, effort, experience, business conditions, and the applicable compensation arrangement.
